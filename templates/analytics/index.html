How Are These KPIs Calculated?
Inventory Turnover Ratio
COGS ÷ Current Inventory at Cost
Measures how many times inventory is sold and replaced during the period. COGS = Σ(units sold × unit cost price). A higher ratio indicates efficient inventory management.
Note: Uses current inventory as proxy for average inventory since historical snapshots are not tracked.
Inventory Days
(Current Inventory at Cost ÷ COGS) × Days in Period
How many days the current stock would last at the current sales rate. Lower values indicate faster-moving inventory.
Dead Stock
Products with current stock > 0 and zero sales in the last {{ kpis.analysis_days }} days. Capital locked is valued at cost price × stock quantity.
Low Stock / Out of Stock / Overstock
Low Stock: stock > 0 AND stock ≤ reorder level
Out of Stock: stock = 0
Overstock: stock > maximum stock level
Inventory Health Score (0–100)
Weighted composite: Turnover (30%) + Stock Availability (25%) + Dead Stock penalty (20%) + Low Stock penalty (15%) + Overstock penalty (10%). Higher scores indicate healthier inventory. ≥ 80 Excellent ≥ 60 Good ≥ 40 Fair < 40 Poor
