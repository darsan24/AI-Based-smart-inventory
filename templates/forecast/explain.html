{# ── 6. What is SHAP? ──────────────────────────────────── #}
What is SHAP?
SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) explains how much each input factor influenced the AI's demand prediction. Instead of showing only the final predicted number, SHAP breaks down why the model arrived at that prediction — revealing which factors pushed demand higher and which pulled it lower. This makes the AI transparent and trustworthy, so you can make informed inventory decisions.
